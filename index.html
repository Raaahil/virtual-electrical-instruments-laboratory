About Virtual Electrical Instruments Laboratory

A focused educational platform for virtual electrical instrumentation and measurement practice.

Project Purpose

The Virtual Electrical Instruments Laboratory is developed as a student-friendly, software-based laboratory environment. Its primary objective is to enable electrical and electronics engineering students to practice instrument operations, parameter range selection, scale interpretations, and circuit calculations without the risk of equipment damage or the requirement for benchtop hardware.

Each of the 12 instruments simulates the calibrated electrical behavior of physical laboratory instruments using accurate mathematical models and deterministic formulas.

Key Objectives

  • Instrument Familiarization: Learn the interface, controls, selectors, and terminal characteristics of fundamental test bench equipment.
  • Measurement Precision: Understand the impact of measurement range selection, resolution, and overload conditions.
  • Zero Physical Constraints: Accessible anytime from any modern web browser without specialized hardware or software installations.
  • Core Law Application: Direct reinforcement of Ohm’s Law, Joule’s Law, power factor relationships, waveform metrics, and circuit impedance principles.

Instrumentation Summary & Governing Principles

No. Instrument Category Governing Relationship / Formula Primary Application
1 Digital Multimeter (DMM) Voltage & Current DCV: V = Vs × R_x / (R1 + R2), DCA: I = Vs / R, Ω: R = V / I Multi-mode testing of divider nodes, loop current, and component continuity
2 Voltmeter Voltage & Current V = Vs × R2 / (R1 + R2) (Voltage divider node measurement) Measuring potential difference across circuit elements from supply Vs
3 Ammeter Voltage & Current I = Vs / R (Ohm's Law series loop current) Measuring series loop current flowing from voltage source Vs through resistance R
4 Wattmeter Power & Energy P = V × I × cos(φ), S = V × I, Q = √(S² - P²) Measuring real active power consumed by AC/DC loads from V, I, and PF
5 Energy Meter Power & Energy Energy = (V × I × PF × Time) / 1000 (kWh) Recording cumulative energy consumed over operating duration in hours
6 Ohmmeter Resistance & Components R = V_test / I_test (Voltage-current test ratio) Measuring unknown component resistance from internal test excitation
7 Oscilloscope (CRO) Waveforms & Signals Vpp = 2 × Vp, Vrms (Sine: Vp/√2, Sq: Vp, Tri: Vp/√3), T = 1 / f Calibrated graticule visualization and automatic parametric measurement
8 Function Generator Waveforms & Signals Signal synthesis (Sine, Square, Triangle, Sawtooth) Benchtop source instrument generating reference AC excitation waveforms
9 Frequency Counter Waveforms & Signals f = N / T_gate, T_period = 1 / f Measuring incoming signal repetition rate from counted cycles N over gate time
10 LCR Meter Resistance & Components L = XL / (2πf), C = 1 / (2πfXC), R = V / I Determining inductance and capacitance from measured AC reactances and frequency
11 Power Factor Meter Power & Energy PF = P / (V × I) or PF = cos(φ) Determining active-to-apparent power ratio and lagging/leading nature
12 DC Power Supply Power Supply I_req = V_set / R_load (CV Mode vs CC Current Clamping Mode) Benchtop source instrument supplying regulated DC voltage and current protection
